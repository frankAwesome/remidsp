01

Dual-engine delay

TAPE BLOOM · A/B ENGINES

Two engines in series or parallel, tempo-synced, with in-loop saturation and compounding HF loss so repeats decay the way tape does. Wow, flutter, diffusion smear, ducking — and feedback that goes past 100 % and blooms instead of screaming.

02

Stereo reverb

8-LINE FDN · GRANULAR SHIMMER

A modulated eight-line feedback delay network with a granular shimmer riding on top — three-dimensional tails that actually bloom, opt-in ducking, and wet-only filters to shape the space without touching the note.

03

Multitrack looper

COUNT-IN · OVERDUBS

Stack layers against a count-in, then ride each one — level and pan per layer, and every take lands on the grid.

04

Studio strip

CONSOLE EQ · FET COMP

A REMI 4000 console EQ into a 2026 FET compressor with a live VU needle. Your tone lands finished, not raw.

The Numbers

CHROME / EDGE RECOMMENDED
  • 3captured heads
  • 6amp voices
  • 10chain modules
  • 2.7ms added latency
  • 0installs
  • 0dollars, ever
Latency
No lookahead and no internal buffering anywhere in the chain — one 128-sample render quantum (~2.7 ms at 48 kHz) plus whatever your device I/O costs. That is the whole budget.
Engine
The NAM capture model runs as SIMD WebAssembly inside an AudioWorklet; the rest of the rig is hand-rolled DSP in a second worklet on the same render thread.
Input
Echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto-gain are all forced off. Pick your interface before you open the rig, or press Listen First and never be asked for a microphone at all.
Captures
Plays any NAM file — the bundled amps, or the whole TONE3000 catalog. Cab IRs load into a zero-latency convolver.
